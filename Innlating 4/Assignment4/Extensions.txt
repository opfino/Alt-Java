Assignment 4 extensions

1. Colorful game elements
Robots, the goal, and teleporters are displayed with ANSI terminal colors. This makes it easier to distinguish the board elements while playing in the console.

2. Teleporter
The Teleporter class is a BoardElement. If a robot lands on one teleporter cell while sliding, it is moved to the paired teleporter cell. If the destination is already occupied by another robot, the teleport does not happen.
